Hot Beans Web was founded in 2010 by Mark Lewis, a web developer with a passion for building great websites. After struggling to find a company that could bring his vision to life, he decided to create his own. The name "Hot Beans Web" came from the philosophy: fresh ideas brewed with energy and flair
In 2013, the business moved from a tiny office in London to a bigger space in Farnborough Business Park. This gave Mark the chance to grow his team and take on more clients. With a focus on creativity and great customer service, Hot Beans Web quickly became a go-to name for web development.
Now, in 2025, we work with a variety of businesses, helping them build the best websites possible. We’re always looking for new talent, so check out our job openings and see if you’d be a great fit for the Hot Beans Web family!
